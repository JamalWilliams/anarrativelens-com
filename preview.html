05. Layout Rules

The "No 90s Frame" Rule

Sections should flow fluidly. Avoid horizontal dividers, card shadows, and hard borders that segment the experience. Use background color shifts and white space to define context.

Cinematic Spacing

Padding starts at `py-24`. Containers are strictly `max-w-7xl`. Heading margins are generous (`mb-10`) to allow typography to breathe.
