**WHY YOU’RE STILL GETTING GHOSTED ON DISCOVERY CALLS**

**Funnels & Conversions**

You finally got someone to book a call — great!

Then… crickets.
No show. No follow-up. Just ghosted.

If this has happened more than once (or ten times), you’re not alone — but it’s not just “how it goes.”

More often than not, discovery call ghosting has less to do with your offer and more to do with your follow-up system.

Let’s fix that.

---

**SOUND FAMILIAR?**

You get the notification: “New Call Booked.”
You prep. You wait. Nothing.

Or maybe they do show up — but they seem unsure, distracted, and don’t convert.

Here’s what’s really happening:

*   They forgot about the call
*   They didn’t feel confident or clear about what to expect
*   They booked impulsively and lost interest
*   They weren’t qualified in the first place

None of these are your fault — but all of them are your responsibility to solve with better systems.

---

**THE FIX: AUTOMATE AND HUMANIZE YOUR FOLLOW-UP FLOW**

High-converting coaches don’t just get booked — they build trust between the booking and the call.

That means:

*   Confirming the call instantly
*   Reinforcing the value
*   Showing professionalism
*   Making the process easy and clear

Think of it like this: every booking is a window. Your follow-up process determines if they walk through it or close it quietly.

---

**PROOF IT WORKS**

Studies show most leads need 6 to 8 touchpoints before they make a decision — especially for service-based offers.

If you’re only touching base once (booking confirmation), you’re giving up before the sale even starts.

The goal isn’t to “remind” them — it’s to build anticipation.

---

**TIP 1: CONFIRM IMMEDIATELY**

As soon as they book, they should get a confirmation email (and ideally a text).

Include:

*   The date + time (with time zone!)
*   What to expect on the call
*   A short video intro or welcome message (if possible)

The faster you confirm, the more trust you build.

---

**TIP 2: ADD VALUE PRE-CALL**

Use your confirmation or reminder message to build credibility before you even meet.

Try:

*   A link to a short “About You” video
*   A free resource (guide, quiz, checklist)
*   A case study or testimonial that relates to them

This shifts the dynamic — they’re not just “checking you out”… they’re seeing your authority in action.

---

**TIP 3: USE REMINDER AUTOMATION**

You are not their calendar.

Set up automated reminders:

*   24 hours before
*   1 hour before
*   Optional: 10 minutes before

Use friendly, casual copy that reinforces the benefit of the call and makes it easy to reschedule if needed.

This drastically cuts down no-shows without extra work on your part.

---

**TIP 4: FOLLOW UP IF THEY NO-SHOW**

Life happens. Ghosting doesn’t always mean “no.”

Instead of writing them off, send a thoughtful, low-pressure message offering:

*   A link to reschedule
*   A 2-question async form or video reply option
*   Or simply ask if they still need support

Sometimes that second invitation is the one that sticks.

---

**BONUS TIP: YOU CAN BE HIGH-TOUCH AND AUTOMATED**

Too many coaches think automation = cold and robotic.

That’s false.

Automation creates consistency — which creates confidence.
You can still add personality and warmth through your words, video, and voice.

The best follow-up systems feel personal while running automatically in the background.
